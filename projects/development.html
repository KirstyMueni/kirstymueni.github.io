Curated Digital Curriculum Development & Offline Learning Project

Project Overview

Supported the development and curation of digital educational content designed for offline access using the Kolibri learning platform within schools supported by Lewa Wildlife Conservancy.

The project focused on improving access to quality digital learning resources for learners and educators in remote areas with limited internet connectivity.

Key Responsibilities

  • Curated and organized offline digital learning content.
  • Configured Kolibri learning platform for offline accessibility.
  • Supported STEM and digital literacy content integration.
  • Organized educational resources for structured learning.
  • Supported teachers in accessing and utilizing digital content.
Key Focus:

Enhancing digital learning accessibility through offline-first educational technology solutions.

Tools & Technologies Used

  • Kolibri Learning Platform
  • Offline Learning Servers
  • VS Code
  • Digital Educational Resources
  • Google Workspace Tools

Impact & Outcomes

  • Improved access to quality digital educational content.
  • Enhanced offline learning capabilities in remote schools.
  • Supported digital literacy and technology integration.
  • Promoted inclusive and accessible learning environments.
