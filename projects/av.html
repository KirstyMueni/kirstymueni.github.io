AV System Design Project

Project Overview

Designed and proposed customized audio-visual solutions tailored to client requirements for conference rooms, training spaces, hospitality environments, and collaborative workspaces.

A key example involved supporting AV installation and classroom technology integration at Three Steers Hotel.

The project focused on delivering efficient, user-friendly, and scalable AV system designs while considering room acoustics, display visibility, ease of use, connectivity, and overall user experience.

Key Focus:

Designing modern and practical AV environments that enhance communication, presentations, collaboration, and digital engagement.

Project Responsibilities

  • Conducted client needs assessments and site evaluations.
  • Designed customized AV system layouts for various environments.
  • Recommended suitable display, audio, and conferencing solutions.
  • Considered room acoustics and viewing angles during planning.
  • Prepared system proposals and equipment recommendations.
  • Supported AV demonstrations and client consultations.
  • Assisted in solution planning for conference and training spaces.
