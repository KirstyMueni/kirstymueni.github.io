Smart Classroom & Conferencing Solutions Project

AV Integration · Video Conferencing & Interactive Displays · Vault Electronics Kenya

Project Overview

Designed and deployed integrated smart classroom and video conferencing solutions for educational institutions and businesses, bringing together interactive displays, audio systems, and remote collaboration tools into a single, seamless environment. The aim was spaces where a lesson or a meeting could flow between in-person and remote participants without friction.

This work sits where AV meets learning and hybrid work: the technology has to be robust enough for daily use and simple enough that a teacher or team lead can run it alone.

Key Responsibilities

  • Designed smart classroom layouts combining displays, audio, and cameras.
  • Integrated high-definition video conferencing with automated controls.
  • Deployed interactive displays for teaching and collaboration.
  • Tuned audio and camera framing for clear remote participation.
  • Configured one-touch control so sessions start without setup fuss.
  • Trained staff and provided handover documentation and support.
Key Focus: Creating hybrid-ready spaces where in-person and remote participants share one seamless, easy-to-run experience.

Impact & Outcomes

  • Hybrid-ready classrooms and meeting rooms delivered for clients.
  • Smooth collaboration between in-person and remote participants.
  • One-touch operation that staff can run without technical help.
  • More engaging, flexible teaching and meeting experiences.
