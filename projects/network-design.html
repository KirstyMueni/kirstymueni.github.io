Network Design & Support Project

Networking · Cisco Packet Tracer · Rasmilink Limited

Project Overview

Assisted in designing and maintaining a basic enterprise network during attachment at Rasmilink Limited. The work combined real-world support with simulated design in Cisco Packet Tracer, modelling a secure, scalable topology and segmenting it with VLANs so that traffic could be organised and controlled from the start.

The goal was a network that was not only connected, but logically structured: easy to understand, easy to extend, and easier to troubleshoot when something went wrong.

Key Focus: Designing a secure, scalable, well-segmented enterprise network that is reliable and easy to maintain.

Key Responsibilities

  • Designed and simulated a secure enterprise network topology in Cisco Packet Tracer.
  • Configured and verified Virtual Local Area Networks (VLANs) on a Cisco switch.
  • Assigned ports and segmented traffic by department and function.
  • Assisted with structured connectivity and hardware configuration on site.
  • Documented the topology for handover and future maintenance.
  • Supported ongoing network stability during the attachment.
