Scratch Programming – Lewa Wildlife Conservancy

Project Overview

Supported STEM learning initiatives at Lewa Wildlife Conservancy through the introduction of Scratch programming to learners in supported schools. The project focused on building foundational computational thinking, creativity, and problem-solving skills through interactive and beginner-friendly coding activities.

Key Focus:

Introducing learners to coding, computational thinking, and STEM concepts through engaging and project-based digital learning experiences.

Key Responsibilities

  • Introduced learners to Scratch visual programming concepts.
  • Facilitated beginner coding and STEM learning sessions.
  • Guided students in creating animations, games, and interactive projects.
  • Supported computational thinking and logical reasoning development.
  • Encouraged creativity and innovation through project-based learning.

Skills & Technologies Used

  • Scratch Programming
  • STEM Education
  • Computational Thinking
  • Digital Literacy
  • Project-Based Learning
  • Educational Technology

Impact & Outcomes

  • Improved learner engagement in STEM-related activities.
  • Introduced foundational coding skills to young learners.
  • Enhanced creativity and logical problem-solving abilities.
  • Increased exposure to digital and computational learning tools.
