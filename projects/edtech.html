Interactive Classroom Technology Integration – Lewa Wildlife Conservancy

Project Overview

Supported the integration of interactive classroom technologies within learning institutions supported by Lewa Wildlife Conservancy to improve digital learning experiences and classroom engagement.

The project focused on introducing and supporting the use of Interactive Flat Panels and digital learning tools to create more collaborative, modern, and technology-driven classroom environments.

Key Focus:

Enhancing digital classroom experiences through interactive educational technologies and collaborative learning tools.

Key Responsibilities

  • Assisted in the setup and configuration of Interactive Flat Panels.
  • Supported educators during technology integration sessions.
  • Demonstrated interactive teaching and presentation tools.
  • Provided basic troubleshooting and technical support.
  • Assisted in digital classroom readiness and device connectivity.

Skills & Technologies Used

  • Interactive Flat Panels
  • Smart Classroom Technologies
  • Educational Technology Support
  • Digital Classroom Integration
  • Technical Support & Troubleshooting
  • Presentation & Collaboration Tools

Impact & Outcomes

  • Improved classroom engagement and learner participation.
  • Enhanced digital teaching and content delivery methods.
  • Increased educator confidence in using classroom technology.
  • Supported the transition toward technology-enabled learning environments.
