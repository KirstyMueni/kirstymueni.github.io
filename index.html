About Me

I specialize in the intersection of IT Support, Cybersecurity, and EdTech. My journey started with a fascination for how systems work and how to fix them when they don't.

With a foundation in technical support and a Cisco Ethical Hacking certification, I bring a "security-first" perspective to every project. I don't just focus on whether a system works; I focus on how it can be hardened against vulnerabilities while remaining seamless for the end-user.

Beyond the hardware and code, I am a passionate advocate for EdTech, STEM education and Digital Literacy, focusing on how technology can improve learning access, digital skills development and innovation in education systems.

Education & Training

Challenge: Improve access to educational materials and bridge the digital divide among learners in underserved communities with limited exposure to technology.

Solution: Curated and deployed offline educational content through digital learning platforms, provided technical support for classroom technology and conducted hands-on training for teachers and students on using digital devices to access and navigate offline learning resources effectively.

Result: Expanded access to quality educational content without internet dependency, successfully empowered over 100 teachers and learners with essential digital skills, improved access to quality educational resources and integration of technology into classroom learning.

Robotics Competition - Ol Pejeta Conservancy

EdTech & STEM

Challenge: To coordinate, evaluate and guide diverse student teams competing in the World Robot Olympiad (WRO) categories, ensuring strict adherence to official international robotics game rules, scoring rubrics and engineering standards.

Solution: Served as an Assistant Judge, systematically scoring autonomous robot runs, verifying mechanical compliance, evaluating algorithmic problem-solving, and providing technical feedback to competitors during the tournament brackets.

Result: Successfully adjudicated competitive matches to ensure fair play, supported the regional STEM pipeline and inspired young teams to advance their engineering and coding skills under official WRO standards.
